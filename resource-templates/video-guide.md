# Acculum — Video Walkthrough Guide

## Recommended Length

Follow the HackMysuru submission requirement for the final video duration.

## 1. Problem — 45 seconds

Explain the Personalized Learning challenge.

Students have different:

- Paces
- Performance levels
- Interests

Facilitators need to understand when and how to intervene.

## 2. Student Experience — 2 minutes

Demonstrate:

1. Student dashboard
2. Subjects/courses
3. Lesson
4. Quiz
5. Progress
6. AI companion

## 3. Personalization — 1.5 minutes

Show how interests affect examples.

Example:

**Probability + Cricket**

Then show how performance changes the recommendation:

- Strong → next topic
- Medium → practice
- Low → revision

## 4. Facilitator Dashboard — 2 minutes

Demonstrate:

- Student overview
- Learning signals
- Repeated errors
- Help-seeking
- Engagement
- Intervention recommendation

## 5. Reassessment — 1 minute

Show the reassessment flow and before/after comparison.

Explain that the intervention loop is:

**Signal → Intervention → Reassessment**

## 6. Architecture & AI — 1 minute

Explain:

- Student application
- Facilitator application
- Rule-based learning signals
- Deterministic AI companion
- Future external LLM integration

## 7. Closing — 30 seconds

Summarize:

**Acculum adapts learning to the student while giving facilitators actionable signals for intervention.**

## Recording Checklist

- Show the working student platform
- Show personalization
- Show the AI companion
- Show facilitator signals
- Show intervention
- Show reassessment
- Explain current AI honestly
- Mention known limitations
- Avoid claiming features that are not implemented
